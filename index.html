A Welcome From Our President

On behalf of the brothers of the Psi Kappa Chapter of Alpha Kappa Psi at San Francisco State University, I want to warmly welcome you to our chapter.

Alpha Kappa Psi is the oldest and largest coeducational professional business fraternity in the world. Founded in 1904 at New York University, our organization now includes more than 298,000 initiated members at 219 universities across four countries. We are dedicated to developing principled business leaders who lead with purpose, character, and vision.

As I look to the future of our chapter, I see tremendous opportunities for academic achievement, leadership growth, and meaningful involvement both on campus and within the community. We aim to provide a space where members can thrive through interactive experiences, professional development, and a shared commitment to personal excellence.

Our fraternity is founded on five core values: Brotherhood, Unity, Service, Integrity, and Knowledge. These values guide our actions, shape our culture, and inspire us to become better students, professionals, and leaders. Through these values, our chapter continues to grow as a strong and supportive community for aspiring business professionals.

It is an honor to serve as President of the Psi Kappa Chapter. I encourage you to explore our website, connect with our members, and learn more about how Alpha Kappa Psi can support you on your journey.

Best Wishes,

Jasmine Lane-Garcia

President, Alpha Kappa Psi

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Brotherhood

Trust, respect, cooperation, companionship and aid to members is the expected norm.

Unity

A common understanding of our vision and values that transcends chapter, generation and professional is utilized to anticipate and create the future.

Service

Sharing of time, talent and treasure with society and within our fraternity is a priority.

Integrity

All actions, whether in business or in life, are guided by honesty, ethics and fairness.

Knowledge

The pursuit of wisdom through the investigation of principles, theories and concepts is a lifelong endeavor.
