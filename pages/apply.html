Apply for Rush

Fall 2026 applications have closed. Here's what to expect for our next round.

Thank you to everyone who came out for Rush Week and applied this semester — we loved getting to know you! Applications for Fall 2026 are now closed while we review candidates. Our next round of recruitment will open for Spring 2027.

Requirements to Join

When applications reopen, you'll be eligible to apply if you are graduating Spring 2027 or later and have a 2.0+ cumulative GPA. If you're a freshman, don't worry about the GPA requirement!

  1. Online Application: fill out the Google Form once it reopens
  2. Cover Letter: a one-page document that introduces you to our fraternity
  3. Resume: a summary of relevant job experience, education, skills, and accomplishments (don't worry, it doesn't need to be perfect)
  4. 1 Google Slide: a slide containing the following information — full name, class standing & graduation year (e.g. Sophomore, Spring 2027), major, where are you from?, where do you see yourself in 5 years?, hobbies and interests, a clear picture of yourself (professional headshot if possible)
  5. Class Schedule: take a screenshot of your weekly schedule

Should you have any questions about the application, interview, or recruitment in general, please email membership.akpsisfsu@gmail.com.
