Applications Due on Friday, September 11th, at 3:00 PM

Thank you for your interest in joining Alpha Kappa Psi! We look forward to getting to know you a little bit better through your application and during your interview. Below is both our application and our necessary qualifications in order to help you be a strong candidate.

Requirements to Join

Please keep in mind, you are only eligible to apply if you are graduating Spring 2027 or later and have a 2.0+ cumulative GPA. If you're a freshman, don't worry about the GPA requirement!

  1. Online Application: fill out the Google Form that is attached below
  2. Cover Letter: a one-page document that introduces you to our fraternity
  3. Resume: a summary of relevant job experience, education, skills, and accomplishments (don't worry, it doesn't need to be perfect)
  4. 1 Google Slide: a slide containing the following information — full name, class standing & graduation year (e.g. Sophomore, Spring 2027), major, where are you from?, where do you see yourself in 5 years?, hobbies and interests, a clear picture of yourself (professional headshot if possible)
  5. Class Schedule: take a screenshot of your weekly schedule

You need to submit all documents in PDF format in the Google Form button below. Should you have any questions about the application, interview, or recruitment in general, please email membership.akpsisfsu@gmail.com.
