The Psi Kappa Chapter

The Psi Kappa Chapter is a student-run professional organization at San Francisco State University open to all genders and majors. Recognized as the premier developer of principled business leaders, we educate our members about the professional environment and expose them to networking events, professional development workshops, and service events to build their character as future business professionals.

Our brotherhood, which stands at 34 members strong, not only fosters a community that strives for professional success but also fosters a community that comes together to create a multitude of like-minded individuals in the midst of an ever-changing San Francisco State University student body. We help prepare our members to enter the professional world by establishing long-lasting relationships with business partners and by fostering business skills. Every year, we produce and provide campus-wide professional events, workshops, and mixers to educate our members about a variety of industries and career-related issues.

Alpha Kappa Psi is much more than just another organization or club — it is a unique, prestigious association of students, professors, graduates, and professionals with common interests and goals. Alpha Kappa Psi provides valuable educational, friendship and networking opportunities.

Our Projects

Signature events and initiatives that define the Psi Kappa Chapter experience.

Launchpad

Each year, our organization hosts our very own networking/recruiting event called Launchpad, which is a unique professional showcase event designed to bring together reputable business leaders with highly ambitious CSU students.

ThinkTank

The Psi Kappa Chapter proudly presents the annual ThinkTank, a professional speaker showcase that features 5 speakers. Our diverse panel will cover an array of topics to educate and inspire the students of San Francisco State.

Upcycling

From turning used coffee grounds into soaps, scrubs, and candles, our Service Committee has developed multiple innovative ideas to diminish waste and to help the environment.

HeartBeats Benefit Concert

The Psi Kappa Chapter is proud to announce our annual Benefit Concert to be held at San Francisco State University. Proceeds go to the Wildlife Conservation Network.
